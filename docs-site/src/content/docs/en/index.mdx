## 4 card types

Each word generates **4 different cards** for complete learning:

1. **Recognition** — You see the Chinese characters and recall the meaning
2. **Translate** — You see the meaning in Spanish/English and recall the Chinese characters
3. **Listening** — You hear the audio and identify the word
4. **Fill in** — You complete the blank in a sentence
