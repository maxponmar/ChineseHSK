## Your daily routine

Dedicate **15-20 minutes a day** to studying with Anki. Consistency matters more than quantity.

### How Anki works

Anki uses a system called **spaced repetition**. The idea is simple:
- Cards you **remember well** are shown less often
- Cards you **find harder** are shown more often
- Over time, everything moves into your long-term memory

### The 4 card types

Each word has 4 different cards:

#### 1. Recognition
- **Front:** You see the Chinese characters (汉字)
- **Back:** Pinyin + meaning in Spanish and English + example sentence + audio
- **Goal:** Learn to recognize Chinese characters

#### 2. Production
- **Front:** You see the meaning in Spanish and English
- **Back:** Chinese characters + pinyin + audio
- **Goal:** Recall how to write/say a word

#### 3. Listening
- **Front:** You only hear the audio (no text)
- **Back:** Characters + pinyin + meaning
- **Goal:** Train your ear for real conversations

#### 4. Fill in the blank
- **Front:** A sentence with a blank + translation
- **Back:** The full sentence with the word highlighted + audio
- **Goal:** Learn to use words in context

### How to rate cards

When you see the answer, Anki gives you 4 options:

| Button | When to use it |
|--------|----------------|
| **Again** (red) | You didn't remember the answer |
| **Hard** | You remembered but with great effort |
| **Good** | You recalled correctly |
| **Easy** | You knew it immediately |

:::tip[Be honest with yourself]
If you're not sure, choose "Again" or "Hard". It's better to review a card one extra time than to forget it.
:::

### Using tags to filter

Cards have tags that let you study by category:

- `HSK1::Lesson3`, `HSK1::Lesson4`, etc. — By lesson
- `daily::greetings`, `daily::food`, etc. — Everyday phrases
- `work::meetings`, `work::tech`, etc. — Work phrases
- `grammar::lesson3`, etc. — Grammar by lesson

To filter: in Anki, click **"Browse"** and use the tags in the sidebar.
