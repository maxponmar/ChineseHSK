## Tips to get the most out of Anki

### 1. Study every day
The key to success is **consistency**. It's better to study 10 minutes every day than 1 hour once a week. Anki works best when you use it daily.

### 2. Study in the morning
Your brain is freshest in the morning. If possible, do your Anki session before starting your day.

### 3. Use headphones for listening cards
The "Listening" card type is very important for training your ear. Use headphones to better hear the pronunciation.

### 4. Don't add too many new cards
Anki lets you configure how many new cards to see per day. Start with **10-15 new cards per day** and gradually increase.

### 5. Study grammar separately
Grammar cards require more concentration. You can use tags to study only grammar in separate sessions.

### 6. Sync between devices
With a free account on **[AnkiWeb](https://ankiweb.net)**, you can sync your progress between your computer and phone:
1. Create an account at ankiweb.net
2. In Anki, click the **"Sync"** button
3. Sign in with your account
4. Repeat on your other device

### 7. Review pending cards first
When you open Anki, first review the cards you've already seen (reviews). Then study new cards.

### 8. Practice with your classmates
Cards are just one part of learning. Practice speaking with your classmates using the phrases you've learned.
