## Frequently asked questions

### Is Anki free?

**On desktop:** Yes, Anki is completely free for Windows, Mac, and Linux. Download it at [apps.ankiweb.net](https://apps.ankiweb.net).

**On Android:** Yes, the app is called **AnkiDroid** and is free on the Play Store.

**On iPhone/iPad:** No, the **AnkiMobile** app costs approximately $24.99 USD. As a free alternative, you can use [ankiweb.net](https://ankiweb.net) in your phone's browser.

### Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Install AnkiDroid (Android, free) or AnkiMobile (iOS, paid). Create an account on [AnkiWeb](https://ankiweb.net) to sync your progress between devices.

### How do I update to a new version of the deck?

Simply download the new `.apkg` file and import it into Anki. Anki will **update** the cards automatically and **preserve all your progress**. You won't lose your reviews or statistics.

### What if I lose my progress?

To avoid this, use **AnkiWeb** to sync your progress. It's free and works as automatic backup. You can also export your collection from Anki as a manual backup (File → Export).

### How many cards should I study per day?

We recommend starting with **10-15 new cards per day**, plus the reviews Anki assigns automatically. As you feel comfortable, you can increase to 20-25.

### Can I customize the cards?

Yes, but we don't recommend it if you're just starting out. The cards are already designed for optimal learning. If you want to make changes, you can edit individual cards from the "Browse" menu in Anki.

### Audio isn't playing — what do I do?

1. Check that your device volume is not muted
2. In Anki, go to **Tools → Preferences** and verify that audio playback is enabled
3. If you use AnkiDroid, check that you've fully synced (audio is downloaded with the sync)

### Do I need internet to study?

No. Once you import the deck and sync, all content (cards, audio, images) is saved on your device. You can study offline.
