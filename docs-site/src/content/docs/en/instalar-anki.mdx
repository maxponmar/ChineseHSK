---
title: Install Anki
description: How to download and install Anki on your computer or phone
---

Anki is a free app for studying with flashcards. It uses a **spaced repetition** system that shows you cards just when you're about to forget them.

## On a computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux)

1. Open your browser and go to **[apps.ankiweb.net](https://apps.ankiweb.net)**
2. Click the **Download** button

![Anki download page](/ChineseHSK/images/ankiweb-download.png)

3. Select the version for your operating system (Windows, Mac, or Linux)
4. Open the downloaded file and install Anki like any other application

:::note[Anki is free on desktop]
The desktop version of Anki is completely free for Windows, Mac, and Linux.
:::

### On Windows

After downloading the installer, double-click the `.exe` file and follow the instructions. When done, you'll see this screen:

![Anki installation complete on Windows](/ChineseHSK/images/windows-anki-install.png)

Click **Finish** to open Anki. You'll see the main screen:

![Anki on Windows](/ChineseHSK/images/windows-anki-main.png)

### On Mac

After downloading the `.dmg` file, open it and drag Anki to your Applications folder. Open Anki and you'll see a screen like this:

![Anki on Mac](/ChineseHSK/images/anki-main.png)

## On Android

1. Open the **Play Store** on your phone
2. Search for **"AnkiDroid"**

![AnkiDroid on the Play Store](/ChineseHSK/images/ankidroid-playstore.png)

3. Install the app (it's free)
4. Open it — you'll see an empty screen

:::note[AnkiDroid is free]
AnkiDroid is the official Android version and is completely free.
:::

## On iPhone or iPad

1. Open the **App Store**
2. Search for **"AnkiMobile Flashcards"**

![AnkiMobile on the App Store](/ChineseHSK/images/ankimobile-appstore.png)

3. The app costs approximately $24.99 USD
4. Install it and open it

:::caution[AnkiMobile is paid on iOS]
The iPhone/iPad version costs money. If you'd rather not pay, you can use Anki for free on your computer and study with **[AnkiWeb](https://ankiweb.net)** in your phone's browser (it's free).
:::
